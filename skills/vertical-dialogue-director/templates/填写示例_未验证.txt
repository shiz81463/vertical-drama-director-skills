【实拍光影规范——高优先级】
PRACTICAL EFFECTS ONLY. Every effect must look physically shot in camera on a film set on 35mm film, never added in post.
Every light lights the faces near it, the light falling off into deep darkness. Nothing glows without lighting the world around it. The afternoon daylight comes through the foyer's west windows as one huge, soft, warm source and lies in long pale-gold panes across the polished marble; a face turned toward the glass is lit warm and clear, anyone with the glass behind them takes a thin bright edge along the hair and shoulders, and the marble, bright enough to hold a reflection, throws the light back up as a soft fill under every chin and into the eyes. Overhead the great crystal ceiling light adds a warm, soft top light and scatters small, sharp glints that shift slowly over skin, satin and glass; the jewelry in the lit glass cases answers with tiny, sharp sparks that barely move, and the far corners of the foyer settle into soft shadow. Every face is lit the way a lead is lit in a high-end drama: a large, soft key from a light that is really in the scene, the shadow side of the face falling away cleanly into shade, a thin edge of light along the hair and shoulders that lifts the person off the background, and a clear catchlight in each eye. The eye always goes to the faces first: they are the most carefully lit thing in the frame, the background falls away behind them into soft focus, and the blacks are deep and clean yet still hold detail.
Air: dust drifts in the window light; shoot past a blurred glass-case edge so that everything feels photographed in one real space.
Real lens and real film: highlights bloom and halate with a red-orange halo, slight chromatic fringing, softer focus at the frame edges, heavy motion blur and step-printed smearing on fast action, film grain and faint scratches; the window and crystal highlights roll off into a soft, creamy bloom while the shadows sink into a deep, clean teal-blue darkness.
Restraint: a few large, motivated effects rather than many small ones. Every effect has a physical cause and a physical consequence.

【空间与视线——最高优先级】
SPACE: Ethan stops the receptionist one pace from the windows, face to face; later he stands alone. 180-degree rule: Ethan is always on the LEFT of frame, the receptionist on the RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between them.
EYELINES: Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and a little down, the receptionist screen-LEFT and a little up; the camera stays at eye height. In singles each eyeline passes just beside the lens.
【戏的逻辑——高优先级】
SCENE LOGIC: Ethan adores W.H. from her work alone and hides how badly he wants to meet her; his hands give him away. Her polite answer turns his impatience into nerves, then into private happiness.
【镜头衔接——高优先级】
FIVE SHOTS, hard cuts only at 3, 7, 11 and 15 s. Each shot opens exactly where the last one ended; no line and no action restarts after a cut.
TALL FRAME: the frame is tall and narrow. The two of them share it in depth, one seen over the other's shoulder, never spread across its width. Faces stay in the upper two thirds of the frame.
UPRIGHT: the camera is always level and the right way up: floor at the bottom, gravity pulling down, heads above feet.
ONE IMAGE: every shot is one continuous image of one real space through one lens: no split screen, no panels, no double exposure.
SHOT AND REVERSE SHOT: both sides at the same size; over the shoulder while they face each other, a clean single once he is alone.
CAMERA FEELS: the camera moves only when a feeling changes and holds still while a feeling is held in; it never zooms.
