【打斗——最高优先级】
ENGLISH ONLY: every spoken word is in American English, never Chinese; background people make only wordless sounds (gasps, breath, grunts).{{有喊话或对白才写： This segment has only these <台词句数，英文> lines, each spoken once, word for word, by the person named; only the speaker's lips move.}}
TONE: fierce and fast from the first frame, everything at real speed; hit-stops last only a few frames and snap back to full speed.
FIGHT LOGIC: {{这一场的打法，英文 3–5 句：谁拿什么、用哪只手（只写这一次）；双方想要的距离（安全区和杀伤区）；主角要去哪、打到哪里才算数；每一招由局面逼出来；谁在什么空档里出手}}
Every named character appears exactly once, and no extra looks like any of them.
EVERY EXCHANGE is complete: intent, attack, dodge or block, impact, body reaction, loss of balance, change of position, next move; the momentum of one move starts the next. No pause lasts longer than half a second. Weapon contact lasts 2 to 4 frames, then they part.
REAL HIT REACTIONS, VIOLENT, each heavier than the last: first the final acceleration, robes and hair dragged back, air pressed ahead of the blow; then the contact, 2 to 4 frames of hit-stop, then {{这一场会被打出来的东西：如 sweat, dust, blood or splinters}} blasting outward and the camera knocked hard; then the transfer of force: the point of contact moves first, the body folds around it, the head whips after it; {{挨打的人}} is hurled far, away from whoever hit them, {{撞到什么：如 crashing through a table and tumbling}}. The face of whoever is hit shows the pain. Nobody recovers instantly.
FACES: {{每个有名字的人一句，从剧设定的人物表演档案里挑（表演 T1、T2），如 The swordsman (宁小贤): calm on the surface; it leaks only from his jaw.}} Before every big move a held breath and a set jaw; whoever is hit shows it in three beats: the breath knocked out, the pain on the face, then pushing through or going down; fear keeps the eyes on the threat. Effort and pain make only wordless sounds: grunts and breath.
【镜头衔接——高优先级】
{{镜头数，英文大写，如 SIX}} SHOTS, hard cuts only at {{切点，如 2, 4, 5, 8 and 11}} s. Every cut is matched on action: each shot opens by continuing the exact movement the last shot ended on, in the same direction, in the same {{地方，如 part of the room}} and light; no action restarts after a cut. 180-degree rule: {{A}} is always on the LEFT, {{B}} on the RIGHT; {{进攻和被打飞的方向，如 the swordsman attacks toward the bandit, up into the depth of the room; anyone who is hit flies away from whoever hit them}}. Never cross the axis. Every shot is a new moment from a new angle; nothing is shown twice.
TALL FRAME: the frame is tall and narrow. The two fighters are not spread across its width: when both are in frame, {{真有高低差才写：A is near the lens, low in the frame, and B is farther back, higher in the frame；两人站在同一地面就写：both stand on the same ground at their real heights, the nearer one larger（P21）}}, or one is above the other. Faces and every point of contact stay in the upper two thirds of the frame. Movement that must be read, a rush, a throw, a fall, runs along the length of the frame, toward or away from the lens or down its height, rather than across its narrow width.
UPRIGHT: the camera is always level and the right way up: the floor at the bottom of the frame, gravity pulling down, heads above feet, even while the camera is knocked and tumbles.
ONE IMAGE: every shot is one continuous image of one real space through one lens: no split screen, no panels, no picture in picture, no double exposure.
VISUAL TENSION: the camera never holds still for more than half a second and never lingers on a face: it moves with the action, rushes in physically, never zooming, whips to each new threat a beat late, is knocked by every blow and tumbles with whoever is thrown.
SPEED IS RELATIVE: when the camera follows a fighter, it keeps pace with them, so they stay sharp while the world tears past: whatever is within a few meters, {{近处的东西，如 pillars, lamp flames, flying splinters}}, flashes past in two or three frames as streaks; {{远一点的东西}} smears along the line of movement; the far background barely moves. The camera only just keeps up: on every turn it lags, overshoots and catches up. Speed leaves traces: {{速度留下的痕迹，如 the wake of each rush tears the dust into curls, and the wind of it flattens robes and hair}}.
