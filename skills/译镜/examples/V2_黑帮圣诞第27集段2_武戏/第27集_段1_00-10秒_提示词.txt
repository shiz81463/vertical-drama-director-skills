【实拍光影规范——高优先级】
PRACTICAL EFFECTS ONLY. Every effect must look physically shot in camera on a film set on 35mm film, never added in post.
Every light lights the faces near it, the light falling off into deep darkness. Nothing glows without lighting the world around it. On the pier, light comes only from three sodium-yellow dock lamps on the container side, with darkness between their pools, and from the cold glow of the lake and the misty city lights on its far shore. Each dock lamp is one sodium-yellow pool on wet planks with a halo of mist around the lamp head; between one pool and the next the pier is nearly black, the lake is a black surface with a few broken reflections, and the city lights across the water are small and soft through the mist. Outdoors every breath shows: a small pale cloud leaves the mouth or nose with each exhale and each spoken phrase, drifts a hand's width, catches whatever light is near and is gone within a second. Whatever the colour of the light, the blacks are the same in every scene: deep, clean and faintly ink-blue, still holding detail in hair and dark wool. Every face keeps a clear catchlight in each eye from a light that is really in the scene, and the eyes are never lost in shadow.
Air: a thin mist off the lake hangs over the pier and fine spray drifts in the wind the whole time; every fast move tears it, and every breath shows; shoot through the soft edge of a rusted railing post, a coat collar or wet hair down one side of the frame so that everything feels photographed in one real space.
Real lens and real film: highlights bloom and halate with a red-orange halo, slight chromatic fringing, softer focus at the frame edges, heavy motion blur and step-printed smearing on fast action, film grain and faint scratches; the dock lamp highlights clip and glow while the shadows sink into a deep ink-blue darkness.
Restraint: a few large, motivated effects rather than many small ones. Every effect has a physical cause and a physical consequence.

【戏的逻辑——高优先级】
FIGHT LOGIC: Ethan has the only blade and needs an arm's length; Vincent has empty hands and can only win inside that reach, so he waits until the knife is at his chest, because moving early would let the blade follow him. The full thrust leaves Ethan's arm locked straight out, his wrist right at Vincent's hand; once the wrist gives way Ethan drops to his knees, and his own weight falling forward is what Vincent uses to put him down. 每一下都省到极点，是职业老手的路数；奥利维亚全看见了，她看见的是一个她不认识的父亲。
【人物与表演——高优先级】
REAL AND VISIBLE: real people, a camera very close; the feeling shows plainly on the face and in the voice, never mimed, never a blank face. THREE LAYERS: a key reaction is a flicker, then the feeling breaking through, and it stays visible. The listener is always acting too, lips closed.
伊森又急又乱，全部的力气都在这一刀里，落空以后只剩惊和疼，牙咬着，只有粗喘。文森特冷、准、省，脸几乎是静的，眼睛先动，身体后动。奥利维亚看呆了，忘了呼吸。全程实速，从出手到按倒三秒左右的劲头：每一下都有来由、接触、受力、失去平衡，接触点先动，身体跟着折；风衣和头发被气流带起，溅起的是真实的水花。

【动作质感——高优先级】
TONE: fierce and fast from the first frame, everything at real speed; hit-stops last only a few frames and snap back to full speed.
EVERY EXCHANGE is complete: intent, attack, dodge or block, impact, body reaction, loss of balance, change of position, next move; the momentum of one move starts the next. No pause lasts longer than half a second. Weapon contact lasts 2 to 4 frames, then they part.
REAL HIT REACTIONS, each heavier than the last: first the final acceleration, clothes and hair dragged back, air pressed ahead of the move; then the contact, 2 to 4 frames of hit-stop, then spray and mist blasting outward and the camera knocked; then the transfer of force: the point of contact moves first, the body folds around it, the head whips after it. The face of whoever is hit shows the pain. Nobody recovers instantly.
FACES: Before every big move a held breath and a set jaw; whoever is hit shows it in three beats: the breath knocked out, the pain on the face, then pushing through or going down; fear keeps the eyes on the threat. Effort and pain make only wordless sounds: grunts and breath.
VISUAL TENSION: the camera moves with the action, rushes in physically, whips to each new threat a beat late, is knocked by every blow, then breathes and settles.
SPEED IS RELATIVE: when the camera follows a fighter, it keeps pace with them, so they stay sharp while the world tears past: whatever is within a few meters, railing posts, lamp poles and drifting spray, flashes past in two or three frames as streaks; whatever is far, the black lake and the misty city lights, smears along the line of movement; the far background barely moves. Speed leaves traces: each rush tears the lake mist into curls and kicks spray off the planks.
Air: when a body moves fast, a visible push of air goes ahead of it, scattering the mist; coat tails and hair are pressed flat before the move lands. A body put down onto the wet planks throws up a burst of spray. The snap of the wrist is a sharp sound only.
The knife: a short plain steel blade; it flashes once in the lamp light as it drops and clatters on the wet planks, then lies still.
【镜头语言——高优先级】
每个镜头都是一台真实摄影机在现场拍下的完整竖幅画面，地平线是平的。镜头有呼吸：手持或稳定器带着随人物呼吸的细微浮动，每一镜有起点、有落点，快慢跟着情绪走，动作落定以后镜头也慢慢停稳。景深分三层：前景虚（栏杆柱、衣角、湿发、肩膀），主体实，后景是化开的灯和湖面。构图用三分法和黄金比例，主体落在一侧三分线上，视线前方留出空间，人物关系靠前后纵深和站、跪、坐的姿态拉开。焦段有变化：广角给空间和冲势，标准镜给关系，长焦给情绪和压缩的灯。每个切点都由一个眼神、一句话或一个动作带出来，下一镜从上一镜停住的地方接着往下走。
Every cut has a reason (a look, a line, a reaction, a movement); each shot opens exactly where the last one ended, and no line and no action restarts after a cut. The camera is always level and the right way up, gravity pulling down, heads above feet. Every shot is one continuous image of one real space, photographed through one lens.
