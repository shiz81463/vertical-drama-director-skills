# 连续性账本：姿态 | 位置 | 手里的东西。上一段的收场要等于这一段的开场。
开场: Wendy | 站着 | 在台口拱门前一步 | 手里一支话筒
开场: Ethan | 坐着 | 在第一排、离台口第二个座位 | 手里没有东西
表演: Wendy (温蒂): calm, speaking with quiet power; it leaks from her eyes, which find one face in the room and stay on it.
表演: Ethan (伊森): seated, holding his breath, proud; it leaks from his eyes, which do not leave her.

空间: Wendy stands one step in front of the arch at the head of the runway; Ethan sits in the front row near the stage end, below her and to her right, about six paces away. 180-degree rule: in her shots Ethan is always off screen-LEFT; in his shots the stage end and Wendy are always off screen-RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between them.
视线: when they look at each other, Wendy looks screen-LEFT and a little down, Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and up.
逻辑: Wendy chooses to say the second half to one person as much as to the room. Ethan knows it is meant for him and does not move.
同框: Wendy stands in it alone with the arch rising behind her; Ethan sits or stands with the front row around him and the runway edge low in the frame.
正反打: Wendy from the front with the arch behind her, Ethan across the runway with the rows behind him, both at the same size, tightening together.
状态: from shot 1, whenever Wendy looks screen-LEFT, her eyes rest on Ethan.
结束: Wendy in close-up facing the room, lips just closing; every line has been spoken and all lips are closed; the applause has not started.
