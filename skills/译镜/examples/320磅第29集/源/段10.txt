同框: The two of them share it in depth, one seen over the other's shoulder, never spread across its width.
正反打: over Ethan's shoulder toward her with the bright gap behind her, over her shoulder toward him with the dim corridor behind him, both at the same size, tightening together.
状态: the roses stay in Wendy's hands.
结束: Ethan in close-up, smiling, eyes screen-RIGHT and a little down on her, the dim corridor behind him; every line has been spoken and all lips are closed; they have not left the wings.
