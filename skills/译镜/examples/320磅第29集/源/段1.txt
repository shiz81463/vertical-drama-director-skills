空间: In the aisle, the plus-size model sits in a makeup chair turned toward the aisle and Ethan stops one pace in front of her; Wendy steps out of a fitting room at the other end, about six paces from him. 180-degree rule: the plus-size model is always on the LEFT of frame and Ethan on the RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between Wendy and Ethan.
视线: the model looks screen-RIGHT and up at Ethan, Ethan looks screen-LEFT and down at her; Wendy looks screen-RIGHT just beside the lens, down the aisle toward Ethan.
逻辑: Ethan has dropped every trace of the arrogant heir and is quietly working to earn Wendy back, though he never says so. Wendy sees it, and her face softens before she means it to.
同框: People share it in depth, never spread across its width; people standing on the same floor keep their real heights, the nearer one larger.
正反打: Ethan is always seen with the aisle and the stage drapes behind him, Wendy always with the fitting-room curtains behind her; both singles at the same size.
状态: from shot 2 the small bottle is in the plus-size model's hand; from shot 4 Wendy stands just outside the fitting room.
结束: Wendy alone in close-up outside the fitting room, the curtains blurred behind her, eyes screen-RIGHT just beside the lens, a small smile; every line has been spoken and all lips are closed; Julian has not come over yet.
