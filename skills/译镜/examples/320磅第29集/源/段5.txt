空间: Wendy stands alone one step in front of the arch at the head of the runway, facing the audience down the runway; the audience is in front of her, below and beyond the lens. 180-degree rule: the arch is always behind her; the camera always stays on the audience side of her and never goes behind the backdrop.

逻辑: Wendy is saying in public what she believed about herself for years. It costs her something, and she lets it show only in her eyes.
同框: Wendy stands in it alone with the arch rising behind her.
正反打: Wendy is shot from the front only, alternating a level close shot and a slightly low three-quarter shot, both with the arch blurred behind her.
状态: from shot 3 her eyes are bright and stay bright.
结束: Wendy in the soft light seen from a little below, lips pressed together, head high, eyes bright; every line has been spoken and all lips are closed; her eyes have not yet gone to Ethan.
