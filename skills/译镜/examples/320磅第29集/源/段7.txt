# 连续性账本：姿态 | 位置 | 手里的东西。上一段的收场要等于这一段的开场。
开场: Wendy | 站着 | 在台口拱门前一步 | 手里一支话筒
开场: Ethan | 坐着 | 在第一排、离台口第二个座位 | 手里没有东西
收场: Ethan | 站着 | 在第一排、离台口第二个座位 | 手里没有东西
表演: Wendy (温蒂): calm, her power rising with each phrase; it leaks from her eyes.
表演: Ethan (伊森): on his feet, proud; it leaks from his eyes and from his hands, which will not stop clapping.

空间: Wendy stands one step in front of the arch at the head of the runway; the audience fills the rows on both sides of the runway; Ethan is in the front row near the stage end. 180-degree rule: in Wendy's shots Ethan is always off screen-LEFT; in Ethan's shot the stage end is always at screen-RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between them.
视线: Wendy looks out over the audience just past the lens; Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and up at her.
逻辑: The last words land on everyone at once. Ethan, who once judged her by her size, is the proudest person in the room.
同框: Wendy stands in it with the arch behind her; seen from behind her, she is a small figure low in the frame with the whole room above.
正反打: Wendy from the front with the arch behind her; the room from high behind her; Ethan across the runway with the rows behind him.
状态: from shot 3 the whole room is on its feet and clapping, and stays so; from shot 4 Ethan is standing.
结束: Ethan on his feet, clapping, eyes screen-RIGHT and up on her, the standing rows behind him; every line has been spoken and all lips are closed; the lights have not dimmed for the finale yet.
