# 连续性账本：姿态 | 位置 | 手里的东西。上一段的收场要等于这一段的开场。
开场: Ethan | 站着 | 在第一排、离台口第二个座位 | 手里没有东西
开场: Wendy | 站着 | 在台口拱门前一步 | 手里一支话筒
表演: Ethan (伊森): on his feet, proud and happy; it leaks from his smile, which he cannot put away.
表演: The reporter (记者): sly and amused, a little mean.
表演: Wendy (温蒂): for the first time, nothing held back; it leaks from her eyes, which crinkle as she laughs.

空间: In the front row near the stage end, Ethan stands among the guests; four seats farther from the stage, one chair stands empty and the reporter sits beside it; Wendy stands one step in front of the arch; the models come out of the arch and down the runway. 180-degree rule: in the front-row shots the stage end is always at screen-RIGHT; in the shots along the runway the arch is always at the top of the frame; the camera never crosses to the other side of the runway.
视线: the reporter looks screen-LEFT at his neighbor; Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and up toward Wendy; Wendy looks out over the room just past the lens.
逻辑: The empty chair belongs to the woman who tried to ruin Wendy; nobody misses her. Wendy, at last, is simply happy.
同框: The runway runs up the frame and people share it in depth, never spread across its width: people on the same floor keep their real heights, the nearer one larger.
正反打: the front row is always seen across the runway from the same side and distance; Wendy from the front with the arch behind her.
状态: the whole room stays on its feet; from shot 2 the models are on the runway in the finale pieces.
结束: Wendy in close-up in front of the arch, laughing openly, the models blurred around her; nobody has spoken; she has not left the stage yet.
