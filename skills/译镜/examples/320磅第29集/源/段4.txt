# 连续性账本：姿态 | 位置 | 手里的东西。上一段的收场要等于这一段的开场。
开场: Wendy | 站着 | 在拱门里面、还没出来 | 手里没有东西
收场: Wendy | 站着 | 在台口拱门前一步 | 手里一支话筒
开场: Ethan | 坐着 | 在第一排、离台口第二个座位 | 手里没有东西
表演: The models (模特们): each with her own walk, unhurried and sure of herself.
表演: Ethan (伊森): in the front row, proud; it leaks from his eyes, which follow every model.
表演: Wendy (温蒂): calm, every movement clean; she walks into the light without hurry.

空间: The runway runs straight from the arched backdrop to the photographers' riser; the models walk from the arch toward the far end; the guests sit in rows on both sides; Ethan sits in the front row near the stage end. 180-degree rule: in the shots along the runway the arch is always at the top of the frame; in the front-row shot the stage end is always at screen-RIGHT; the camera never crosses to the other side of the runway.
视线: the models look straight ahead down the runway, just past the lens; the front-row guests look screen-RIGHT and up at the runway; Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and up at the runway; the host looks toward the arch.
逻辑: The audience came half expecting a novelty and is won over model by model. Wendy is about to stand in the light as herself.
同框: The runway runs up the frame and people share it in depth, never spread across its width: people on the same floor keep their real heights, the nearer one larger.
正反打: the runway is always seen from the far end with the arch at the top of the frame; the front row is always seen across the runway from the other side, at the same distance each time.
状态: from shot 1 the house lights are down and the runway is lit; from shot 4 Wendy stands in the spotlight with the microphone and the host is out of the light.
结束: Wendy alone in the soft spotlight one step in front of the arch, microphone in hand, the host off to the side in the dark; nobody has spoken; she has not begun her speech.

== 镜头：灯一暗一亮，开秀 ==
机位: 大全景 35mm | 1 | T 台尽头的摄影师小台上、比人眼高，顺着 T 台往台口看；T 台从画面下方一路通到上方的拱门

声: The room going quiet.
