表演: Ethan (伊森): sincere and a little nervous; it leaks from his eyes, which do not leave her face, and from his hands on the roses.
表演: Wendy (温蒂): warm and open now; it leaks from her eyes, which smile before her mouth does.

空间: In the narrow wings, Ethan stands near the corridor end facing the gap in the drapes; Wendy stands one pace in front of him with her back to the gap. 180-degree rule: Ethan is always on the LEFT of frame and Wendy on the RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between them.
视线: when they look at each other, Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and a little down, Wendy looks screen-LEFT and a little up; the camera stays at their eye height.
逻辑: Ethan does not make a show of it; the roses are all he dares. Wendy, who once waited ten years for him, is the one who asks this time.
同框: The two of them share it in depth, one seen over the other's shoulder, never spread across its width.
正反打: over Ethan's shoulder toward her with the bright gap behind her, over her shoulder toward him with the dim corridor behind him, both at the same size, tightening together.
状态: from shot 3 the roses are in Wendy's hands and stay there.
结束: Wendy in close-up holding the white roses, eyes on him, the question just asked, lips closing; every line has been spoken and all lips are closed; Ethan has not answered yet.
