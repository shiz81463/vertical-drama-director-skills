【实拍光影规范——高优先级】
PRACTICAL EFFECTS ONLY. Every effect must look physically shot in camera on a film set on 35mm film, never added in post.
Every light lights the faces near it, the light falling off into deep darkness. Nothing glows without lighting the world around it. In the wings, light comes only from the stage light pouring through the gap in the drapes and one blue work light clipped to a pipe. Every face is lit the way a lead is lit in a high-end drama: a large, soft key from a light that is really in the scene, the shadow side of the face falling away cleanly into shade, a thin edge of light along the hair and shoulders that lifts the person off the background, and a clear catchlight in each eye. The eye always goes to the faces first: they are the most carefully lit thing in the frame, the background falls away behind them into soft focus, and the blacks are deep and clean yet still hold detail.
FACING 1 (toward the stage gap): the bright warm-white stage light pours through the gap behind whoever faces the camera and draws a strong bright edge on hair and shoulders; the face takes a soft warm key from screen-RIGHT, stage light spilling around the edge of the drape, as bright and clear as every other face in the scene, with a touch of cool blue from the work light at screen-LEFT; the only light source in frame is the glowing gap.
FACING 2 (toward the backstage corridor): the warm stage glow comes from behind the camera and lights faces softly from the front; the blue work light at screen-RIGHT draws a thin cool edge; the corridor behind stays dim.
Air: a faint haze from the stage drifts in through the gap in the drapes; shoot through the soft edge of a black drape down one side of the frame so that everything feels photographed in one real space.
Real lens and real film: highlights bloom and halate with a red-orange halo, slight chromatic fringing, softer focus at the frame edges, heavy motion blur and step-printed smearing on fast action, film grain and faint scratches; the stage highlights roll off into a soft, creamy bloom while the shadows sink into a deep, clean teal-blue darkness.
Restraint: a few large, motivated effects rather than many small ones. Every effect has a physical cause and a physical consequence.

SPACE: In the narrow wings, Ethan stands near the corridor end facing the gap in the drapes; Wendy stands one pace in front of him with her back to the gap. 180-degree rule: Ethan is always on the LEFT of frame and Wendy on the RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between them.
EYELINES: when they look at each other, Ethan looks screen-RIGHT and a little down, Wendy looks screen-LEFT and a little up; the camera stays at their eye height. In singles each eyeline passes just beside the lens. Every look has a target in the scene.
【戏的逻辑——高优先级】
SCENE LOGIC: Ethan does not make a show of it; the roses are all he dares. Wendy, who once waited ten years for him, is the one who asks this time.
【镜头衔接——高优先级】
FOUR SHOTS, hard cuts only at 3, 8 and 10 s. Every cut has a reason (a look, a line, a reaction, a movement); each shot opens exactly where the last one ended, and no line and no action restarts after a cut.
TALL FRAME: the frame is tall and narrow. The two of them share it in depth, one seen over the other's shoulder, never spread across its width. Faces stay in the upper two thirds of the frame; anyone who comes or goes moves toward or away from the lens.
UPRIGHT: the camera is always level and the right way up: floor at the bottom, gravity pulling down, heads above feet.
ONE IMAGE: every shot is one continuous image of one real space through one lens: no split screen, no panels, no picture in picture, no double exposure.
SHOT AND REVERSE SHOT: over Ethan's shoulder toward her with the bright gap behind her, over her shoulder toward him with the dim corridor behind him, both at the same size, tightening together.
CAMERA FEELS: the camera moves only when a feeling changes, eases in as someone gives themselves away, and holds still while a feeling is held in; it never zooms and never cuts away before a reaction lands.
