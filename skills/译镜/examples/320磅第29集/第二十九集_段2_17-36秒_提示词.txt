【实拍光影规范——高优先级】
PRACTICAL EFFECTS ONLY. Every effect must look physically shot in camera on a film set on 35mm film, never added in post.
Every light lights the faces near it, the light falling off into deep darkness. Nothing glows without lighting the world around it. Backstage, light comes only from the warm round bulbs framing the vanity mirrors, small brass lamps on the jewelry table, a few dim warm work lights overhead and one thin cool line of stage light at the gap in the drapes. Every face is lit the way a lead is lit in a high-end drama: a large, soft key from a light that is really in the scene, the shadow side of the face falling away cleanly into shade, a thin edge of light along the hair and shoulders that lifts the person off the background, and a clear catchlight in each eye. The eye always goes to the faces first: they are the most carefully lit thing in the frame, the background falls away behind them into soft focus, and the blacks are deep and clean yet still hold detail.
FACING 1 (toward the stage drapes): the warm vanity bulbs along the left wall light every face from screen-LEFT, soft and even, the bulbs themselves glowing along the left edge of the frame; the far end falls into darkness except one thin cool blue-white line of stage light at the gap in the drapes.
FACING 2 (toward the fitting rooms): the warm vanity bulbs light every face from screen-RIGHT, soft and even, only a few bulbs at the right edge of the frame; the ivory curtains behind glow softly under two small warm spotlights.
FACING 3 (toward the garment racks): the vanity bulbs are behind the camera and light faces softly from the front; the small brass lamps on the jewelry table put tiny sharp sparks on the jewels; no bare bulb is in frame.
Air: a faint haze of face powder and hairspray hangs in the bulb light; shoot through the soft edge of a garment rack down one side of the frame so that everything feels photographed in one real space.
Real lens and real film: highlights bloom and halate with a red-orange halo, slight chromatic fringing, softer focus at the frame edges, heavy motion blur and step-printed smearing on fast action, film grain and faint scratches; the bulb highlights roll off into a soft, creamy bloom while the shadows sink into a deep, clean teal-blue darkness.
Restraint: a few large, motivated effects rather than many small ones. Every effect has a physical cause and a physical consequence.

CAMERA FEELS: the camera moves only when a feeling changes, eases in as someone gives themselves away, and holds still while a feeling is held in; it never zooms and never cuts away before a reaction lands.
