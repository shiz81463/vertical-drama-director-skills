【全局视觉风格】
暖黄朦胧，胶片质感，90年代香港艺术电影，王家卫式唯美古风，杜可风摄影，琥珀金调，柔焦，光晕，高光溢出，烟雾，水汽，雨雾，烛光，油灯，胶片颗粒，轻微划痕，暗角，浅景深，变形宽银幕，2.39:1，35mm，慢门，运动模糊，抽帧，梦幻，诗意，东方古风，情绪化，非现代，非数字，非仙侠网游。
【画面质感——高优先级】
像一部用35mm胶片实拍的电影，不是CG动画：皮肤有毛孔和水珠，湿透的布料贴在身上、有分量，羽毛被雨打湿成一缕一缕；所有东西都笼在同一种暖黄琥珀色的光里，暗部压深、带一点青绿。
【实拍特效规范——高优先级】
PRACTICAL EFFECTS ONLY. Every effect must look physically shot in camera on a film set on 35mm film, never added in post.
1. The sky overhead is dark as night. Light comes only from the two burning wheels far below, a thin band of sunset low on the far horizon, and sparks. Every flame and spark lights its surroundings with an uneven orange flicker: rain, faces, wet feathers and skin, iron, steam, the underside of the clouds and the wet air itself, the light falling off into deep darkness. Nothing glows without lighting the world around it. The image stays warm amber overall: the firelight dominates.
2. Rain and matter in the air: dense rain falls at an angle the whole time in uneven curtains, each drop a thin amber streak where the firelight catches it; it sheets off robes, feathers, skin and iron and sprays off everything that moves, and every impact throws a burst of spray along with whatever it breaks loose. Steam rolls and billows, lit orange from below. Mist and torn cloud drift between the camera and the fighters, some of it right across the lens, and raindrops hit the lens. Shoot through foreground layers (rain, drifting cloud, an out-of-focus chain or knuckle of the giant) so that everything feels photographed in one real space.
3. Air and clouds: real clouds are lumpy, torn and uneven, their wispy edges always dissolving. When something huge moves, a visible wall of wind goes ahead of it, flattening the rain, ripping the clouds into trenches and craters and shoving anything small aside; robes, hair and feathers are pressed flat before the blow lands: the air arrives before the blow. Torn clouds roll back in heavy billows over two to three seconds. Around the giant, cloud, smoke and spray roll with the heavy timing of real storm clouds, fine detail churning at their edges, while rain, sparks and feathers whip past fast.
4. Each kind of contact has its own effect. Sparks come only where steel meets iron: a short burst of separate molten-iron sparks of uneven size that fly apart in arcs under gravity, cool from white to orange to dull red and die in the rain. The giant's skin is flesh, soft and wet under its cracks: a cut opens a thin split that hisses steam and leaks dark blood, washed down by the rain. A blast of air shows as rain and torn cloud jetting outward.
5. The sword slash: the jade sword is plain, wet, green jade-coloured steel; rain sheets off its edge and sprays off whenever it moves. Every swing cuts the curtain of rain open along its arc and leaves a broad crescent-shaped smear: a translucent sheet of rain spray and mist filling the whole area the sword sweeps, dense at the leading edge, gone within a third of a second; never a thin line.
6. Fire: real, dirty, turbulent fire, white-yellow at the core, orange at the edges, curling into thick soot-black smoke, the tips semi-transparent and flickering, torn sideways by wind and rain; where rain hits fire it hisses into white steam. The wheels burn like huge oil fires under rippling heat haze.
7. The giant is always seen through rain, mist and steam, partly hidden, lit from below by the fire at his feet; his wet cracked skin steams where the rain hits it; there is always thick air between the camera and him, so he is never clean and never fully sharp; the farther each part of him, the paler and softer.
8. Real lens and real film: highlights bloom and halate with a red-orange halo, slight chromatic fringing, softer focus at the frame edges, heavy motion blur and step-printed smearing on fast action, film grain and faint scratches; the fire highlights clip and glow while the shadows sink into a deep amber-green darkness.
9. Restraint: a few large, motivated effects rather than many small ones. Every effect has a physical cause and a physical consequence.

【场景：云海之上】
脚下是一望无际、不停翻滚的云海，头顶压着暗得像夜的风暴云，远处左边天边剩一线金色的夕光，斜雨一直在下。巨魔站在右边的云海里，脚下两只燃烧的火轮把附近的云从下往上映成暗橙色。整段都在这一片天空里打，不换地点。
SKY ONLY: no mountains, no rocks, no buildings, no ground. Only the sea of clouds, the storm ceiling, rain, fire, the giant, the rider and the bird.
【人鸟一体——高优先级】
ONE RIDER, ALWAYS MOUNTED: exactly one rider and one blue bird in every frame. The rider sits on its back the whole time, one hand on its neck, the other on the jade sword; when they are hit and lurch, he clings on with his knees. The bird is always flying at full speed, wings beating hard or swept back; every roll and dive kicks it to full speed in two or three frames; the rain bends into its wake, never a straight trail. It never perches on or grips anything and never touches the giant or his trident: only the jade sword does. Every dodge is a full-body reflex: eyes first, then head, then the rider flattens onto the bird's neck, knees clamped, hair and sleeves lagging.
【体型与比例——最高优先级】
SCALE CONTROL: the giant is 700 meters tall and always bigger than the frame; the rider on the bird, about 9 meters long, is about a hundredth of his height. Every shot uses one of two framings. CLOSE ON THE BIRD: the giant appears only as a surface that runs out past the edges of the frame, such as a wall of cracked skin, chain links each bigger than the bird, the flank of one tine, his brow above an eye bigger than the bird, or his open mouth with teeth taller than the rider; never a whole hand, a whole head or the whole trident head beside a large bird. FAR FROM THE BIRD: when a whole part of the giant is in frame (his forearm and fist, his head and shoulders, the trident head), the bird is a fleck smaller than one of his fingernails. Never shrink the giant or his trident to fit a shot.
【打斗——高优先级】
TONE: fierce and fast from the first frame, everything at real speed; hit-stops last only a few frames and snap back to full speed.
FIGHT LOGIC: the giant grips the black iron trident in one fist; his other hand is empty. Close against the giant's body is the rider's safe zone: there the giant cannot see the bird or bring the trident to bear, so he has to slap at his own arm or roar. The open air at trident range is the giant's killing zone: each time the bird is blasted out there, the trident comes, and the rider must fight his way back in. The giant's big moves start with a short, heavy wind-up because he is huge, then come fast and unstoppable, his hand crossing the whole frame faster than the bird can turn; his small moves, a twitch, a turn of the head, a roar, are fast. The rider moves at bird speed and strikes in the gaps; his cuts are shallow to the giant, except near the eye.
EVERY EXCHANGE is complete: intent, attack, dodge or block, impact, body reaction, loss of balance, change of position, next move; the momentum of one move starts the next. No pause lasts longer than half a second. Weapon contact lasts 2 to 4 frames, then they part.
REAL HIT REACTIONS, VIOLENT, each heavier than the last: first the final acceleration, robes, hair and feathers dragged back, air pressed ahead of the blow; then the contact, 2 to 4 frames of hit-stop, then rain, feathers, blood or steam blasting outward and the camera knocked hard; then the transfer of force: the point of contact moves first, the body folds around it, the head whips after it; rider and bird are hurled far, tumbling. The rider's face shows the pain. Nobody recovers instantly. When the giant is cut, the cut part jerks away, then the jolt runs through him.
【镜头衔接——高优先级】
SIX SHOTS, hard cuts only at 1, 4, 7, 10 and 13 s. Every cut is matched on action: each shot opens by continuing the exact movement the last shot ended on, in the same direction, in the same patch of sky and light; no action restarts after a cut. 180-degree rule: the giant is always on the RIGHT facing LEFT; the rider attacks left to right and is thrown right to left. Never cross the axis. Every shot is a new moment from a new angle; nothing is shown twice.
VISUAL TENSION: the camera never holds still for more than half a second and never lingers on a face: it flies with the action, rushes in physically, never zooming, whips to each new threat a beat late, is knocked by every blast and tumbles with the bird.
SPEED IS RELATIVE: when the camera follows the bird, it keeps pace with it, so the bird stays sharp while the world tears past it: whatever is within a few meters, rain, cloud wisps, chain links, the giant's skin, flashes past in two or three frames as streaks; mist and spray tens of meters off smear along the flight path; the giant's far-off bulk barely moves. The camera only just keeps up: on every turn it lags, overshoots and catches up, shaking in the buffeting wind. Speed leaves traces: the bird's wake tears the mist into curls, the headwind flattens the rider's robes and hair, and raindrops on the lens are dragged sideways.
【本段战损】
BATTLE DAMAGE: at the start nobody is hurt. Each wound appears only at the moment it is dealt, then stays to the end: a snapped link in the chain on the giant's trident wrist; a thin split near his trident elbow; a split across his brow, that eye shut and bleeding; a thin red cut on the rider's cheek; the rider's sword arm numb and shaking; flight feathers torn from the bird's wings.
