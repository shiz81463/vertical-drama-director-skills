【实拍光影规范——高优先级】
PRACTICAL EFFECTS ONLY. Every effect must look physically shot in camera on a film set on 35mm film, never added in post.
Every light lights the faces near it, the light falling off into deep darkness. Nothing glows without lighting the world around it. On the pier, light comes only from a row of sodium-yellow dock lamps, with darkness between their pools, and from the cold glow of the lake and the misty city lights on its far shore. Each dock lamp is one sodium-yellow pool on wet planks with a halo of mist around the lamp head; between one pool and the next the pier is nearly black, the lake behind is a black surface with a few broken reflections, and the city lights across the water are small and soft through the mist. Outdoors and in unheated places every breath shows: a small pale cloud leaves the mouth or nose with each exhale and each spoken phrase, drifts a hand's width, catches whatever light is near and is gone within a second; indoors in the warm there is none. Whatever the colour of the light, the blacks are the same in every scene: deep, clean and faintly ink-blue, still holding detail in hair and dark wool. Every face keeps a clear catchlight in each eye from a light that is really in the scene, and the eyes are never lost in shadow.
FACING 2 (toward the landward end of the pier): the cold glow of the lake lights the face pale and cool from screen-RIGHT, soft, with a cold catchlight in each eye; a dock lamp's yellow pool lies on the planks far behind, its lamp head small in frame.
FACING 3 (toward the railing and the lake): the dock lamp above and behind the camera lights the face a soft sodium-yellow warm from above; the black lake behind holds only broken reflections.
Air: a thin mist off the lake hangs over the pier and fine spray drifts in the wind; shoot through the soft edge of a rusted railing post or the corner of a container down one side of the frame so that everything feels photographed in one real space.
Real lens and real film: highlights bloom and halate with a red-orange halo, slight chromatic fringing, softer focus at the frame edges, heavy motion blur and step-printed smearing on fast action, film grain and faint scratches; the dock lamp highlights clip and glow while the shadows sink into a deep ink-blue darkness.
Restraint: a few large, motivated effects rather than many small ones. Every effect has a physical cause and a physical consequence.
