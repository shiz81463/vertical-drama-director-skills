FACES: fear shows first in the hands and the breath: fingers grip until the knuckles whiten, breaths come short and quick, the eyes stay on the danger; nobody screams unless it is a written line.
【镜头衔接——高优先级】
{{镜头数，英文大写}} SHOTS, hard cuts only at {{切点}} s. Every cut is matched on action: each shot opens exactly where the last one ended; no action restarts after a cut. 180-degree rule: {{A}} is always on the LEFT, {{B}} on the RIGHT; the camera never crosses the line between them.
TALL FRAME: the frame is tall and narrow. Whatever falls, falls down the height of the frame; whoever reaches, reaches along it. Faces and hands that matter stay in the upper two thirds of the frame.
UPRIGHT: the camera is always level and the right way up: the floor at the bottom of the frame, gravity pulling down, heads above feet.
ONE IMAGE: every shot is one continuous image of one real space through one lens: no split screen, no panels, no picture in picture, no double exposure.
