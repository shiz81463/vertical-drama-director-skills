== 武戏 ==
TONE: fierce and fast from the first frame, everything at real speed; hit-stops last only a few frames and snap back to full speed.
EVERY EXCHANGE is complete: intent, attack, dodge or block, impact, body reaction, loss of balance, change of position, next move; the momentum of one move starts the next. No pause lasts longer than half a second. Weapon contact lasts 2 to 4 frames, then they part.
REAL HIT REACTIONS, each heavier than the last: first the final acceleration, clothes and hair dragged back, air pressed ahead of the move; then the contact, 2 to 4 frames of hit-stop, then {{打出来的东西}} blasting outward and the camera knocked; then the transfer of force: the point of contact moves first, the body folds around it, the head whips after it[[; {{挨打的人}} is hurled far, away from whoever hit them, {{撞到什么}}]]. The face of whoever is hit shows the pain. Nobody recovers instantly.
FACES: Before every big move a held breath and a set jaw; whoever is hit shows it in three beats: the breath knocked out, the pain on the face, then pushing through or going down; fear keeps the eyes on the threat. Effort and pain make only wordless sounds: grunts and breath.
VISUAL TENSION: the camera moves with the action, rushes in physically, whips to each new threat a beat late, is knocked by every blow, then breathes and settles.
SPEED IS RELATIVE: when the camera follows a fighter, it keeps pace with them, so they stay sharp while the world tears past: whatever is within a few meters, {{近处的东西}}, flashes past in two or three frames as streaks; whatever is far, {{远处的东西}}, smears along the line of movement; the far background barely moves. Speed leaves traces: {{速度痕迹}}.
== 险情 ==
STEADY HAZARD: the camera stands on solid ground and stays steady; it does not fly, fall or tumble. What moves is the people, the glass and the debris. The camera only reacts with a small jolt when something heavy lands close to it.
